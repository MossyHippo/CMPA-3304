Top 5 List

This is my top 5 list of horror films with strong female leads:

  1. Martyrs (2008)

    Pascal Laugier's brutal French film follows Lucie and Anna, two women bound together by childhood abuse and determined to face down the people responsible.

  2. Audition (1999)

    Takashi Miike spends an hour disguising this as a quiet romance before Asami takes over the film completely in one of the great slow-burn turns in horror.

  3. Hereditary (2018)

    Toni Collette plays Annie Graham, a mother whose grief after a death in the family carries the whole film long before the supernatural takes hold.

  4. The Seasoning House (2012)

    Angel, a deaf girl trapped in a Balkan war-time brothel, learns the building better than her captors do and uses it against them.

  5. I Spit on Your Grave (2010)

    A hard-to-watch remake of the 1978 original in which Sarah Butler's Jennifer Hills drives the entire second half of the film.
