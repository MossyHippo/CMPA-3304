Meet Liz

I'm the founder of Deepwood Labs and a student of Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence. I started Deepwood Labs because I believe AI should make people more capable, not less, and the best way to get there is to start with the people who will use it.

How I work

I lead by serving. In practice that means I ask who a tool is for before I ask what it can do, I bring the people affected into the design, and I treat privacy and accessibility as starting points rather than features to add later.

Background

My career is in project management and process improvement: finding where work breaks down for people and redesigning it so it doesn't. Now I'm bringing that same lens to AI through my studies in Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence.
