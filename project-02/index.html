Featured Projects

Amigo

An AI music companion that brings music education to kids who have been left out of it, with offline use, solar charging, privacy built for minors, and lessons shaped by each child's culture.

Bakery

A browser extension concept that shows who is taking your data as customers in your own bakery, so invisible trackers become easy to see and privacy wins feel rewarding.

Magic

A companion app concept that tracks Magic: The Gathering triggers and board state so neurodivergent players can stay in the game, with a "UI loudness" slider that lets each player choose how much help they get.
